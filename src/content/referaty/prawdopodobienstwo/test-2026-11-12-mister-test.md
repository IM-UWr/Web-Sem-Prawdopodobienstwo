Let $X(t)$ be a centred Gaussian process with stationary increments and let $T$ be a random time independent of $X$. We derive exact asymptotics of $\mathbb{P}\left(\sup_{t \in [0,T]} X(t) > u\right)$ as $u \to \infty$ for a wide class of distributions of $T$, covering both light- and heavy-tailed horizons. The answer exhibits three qualitatively different regimes depending on the interplay between the tail of $T$ and the variance function of $X$. Applications include ruin probabilities in fluid queues with random service interruptions and an extension of the Pickands–Piterbarg constants.
